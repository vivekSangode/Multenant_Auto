## 1. Summary

1. **Bundles** (closed under mandatory dependencies).
2. **Plans, immutable versions, monthly/yearly prices**, contract versions (`is_custom` + `custom_company_id`), USER_SEAT and STORAGE plans.

**Not in R1** (CD-10): catalog discounts, coupons, redemptions (PHASE_14). The engine interface accepts them later without change.
