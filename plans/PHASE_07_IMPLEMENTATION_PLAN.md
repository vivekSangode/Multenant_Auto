## 1. Summary

Admin-panel commercial flow of R1: billing account → order (DRAFT → QUOTED → AWAITING_PAYMENT) with a PENDING subscription → **full manual payment** with one or several instruments and verification → at **Σ CONFIRMED = grand total**, one locked activation transaction (order PAID, invoice ISSUED_PAID, subscription ACTIVE, entitlements, FULFILLED) → platform email. Plus manual renewal, credit notes, PDFs, company read APIs and the **platform mail sender** (single static platform mailbox, CD-23).

Size estimate: 11 tasks, about 90–110 files, about 35 test classes (state machines, concurrency, golden PDFs).
