## 1. Summary

1. **Order-driven subscriptions:** several per company, one billing period each, items with plan-version price snapshots, periods (renewal history), append-only events; states PENDING, ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, EXPIRED, CANCELLED (TRIAL and PAST_DUE reserved, unused in R1).
2. **Lifecycle scheduler:** 7-day and 1-day warnings, EXPIRED at `end_date` (0-day grace), scheduled downgrades/cancellations, manual renewal hook; never creates or charges renewals.
3. **Entitlement engine:** materialized `tbl_company_entitlement` from all ACTIVE subscriptions (plan modules, expanded bundles, dependencies, platform core, manual grants) with the **charged guard**.
4. **Allowances** (USERS, STORAGE_BYTES) and usage counters (maintained later by PHASE_09/10).
5. **Publication:** `COMPANY_MODULES:<CMP_n>` (the LEGACY `ORG_MODULES` key was already renamed in every reader and writer in P02-T11 stage B, CD-26 / CD-31) and `TENANT_STATUS:<CMP_n>`, resync on by default; gateway status check.
6. **Company read APIs**; removal of the key/value plan entitlements and `SubscriptionResolver`.

Size estimate: 8 tasks, about 50–65 files across CP, COMMON and the gateway; about 20 test classes.
